# **WI – FLEY Control Manifold**

## **1 Purpose**

This Work Instruction defines how Floating Eye Software (FLEY) prepares, maintains, and uses **Control Manifold Reports** as organization-level situational assessments supporting:

* Management Review preparation
* Risk and Opportunity Management
* Quality Planning
* Leadership activities
* Portfolio governance
* Cross-repository coordination
* Organizational continual improvement

The Control Manifold provides a shared representation of the organization's current situation and serves as a structured input into Management Review and related QMS processes.

This Work Instruction does not establish new approval workflows, action tracking mechanisms, or controlled decision records. Those activities remain governed by applicable SOPs and Work Instructions.

---

## **2 Scope**

### **Applicability**

This Work Instruction applies to:

* Organization-level situational assessment
* Periodic organizational readouts
* Management Review preparation
* Strategic initiatives
* Significant organizational changes
* Organizational risks and opportunities
* Resource and capability assessment
* Cross-repository governance activities

### **Exclusions**

This Work Instruction does not apply to:

* Repository-local execution tracking
* Todo management
* Project execution management
* Controlled Management Review records
* Change approvals
* CAPA management
* Operational issue tracking
* Repository-local workflow procedures

## **4 Definitions**

| Term                        | Definition                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Control Manifold**        | A human-maintained projection of the organization's current situation used to support governance and decision-making activities. |
| **Control Manifold Report** | A dated realization of the Control Manifold template representing the organization's situation during a defined period.          |
| **Situational Surface**     | A section of the Control Manifold representing a major dimension of organizational state, pressure, or capability.               |
| **Operational Telemetry**   | Repository-level observations and execution information that may inform a Control Manifold Report.                               |
| **Management Review Input** | Information provided to Management Review for evaluation of the continuing suitability, adequacy, and effectiveness of the QMS.  |

## **6 Conceptual Model**

The Control Manifold exists between operational execution and governance decision-making.

```text
Repository Execution
        ↓
Operational Telemetry
        ↓
Control Manifold Report
        ↓
Management Review
        ↓
Actions and Decisions
        ↓
Execution
```

The Control Manifold serves as an organizational situation assessment.

It informs decisions but does not itself constitute decisions.

---

## **7 Control Manifold Principles**

The Control Manifold shall:

* represent the current organizational situation
* remain concise and management-oriented
* emphasize changes, pressures, and opportunities
* support multiple QMS processes using a common situational surface
* distinguish observations from decisions
* avoid duplication of authoritative records
* maintain traceability to supporting information where appropriate

---

## **8 Information Sources**

Information contributing to a Control Manifold Report may include:

* repository operational status information
* project and portfolio information
* risks and opportunities
* quality objectives and metrics
* process performance information
* organizational capability information
* external developments
* stakeholder feedback
* prior Management Review outputs
* lessons learned and Verification of Effectiveness activities

Information sources may be maintained in repositories, reports, planning artifacts, issues, controlled documents, or other authoritative organizational records.

---

## **9 Control Manifold Reports**

A Control Manifold Report should assess, as applicable:

1. Status of actions from previous Management Reviews.
2. Significant external developments.
3. Significant internal developments.
4. Organizational resources and capabilities.
5. Strategic fronts and areas of organizational attention.
6. Risks and threats.
7. Opportunities and improvement areas.
8. Dependencies and readiness states.
9. Process and quality-system health.
10. Publications, releases, and significant deliverables.
11. Unresolved strategic questions.
12. Significant changes since the previous report.

The report should emphasize:

* changes
* pressures
* opportunities
* resource constraints
* emerging concerns
* areas requiring management attention

---

## **10 Triggers**

A Control Manifold Report may be prepared:

* periodically
* before Management Reviews
* following significant organizational events
* upon completion of major initiatives
* following significant risks or incidents
* upon achievement of readiness milestones
* when requested by Top Management

Organizations may maintain manifold reports continuously or generate them for specific review activities.

---

## **11 Relationship to Management Review**

Control Manifold Reports serve as structured Management Review inputs.

They may be used to:

* summarize organizational situation
* identify topics requiring review
* highlight risks and opportunities
* identify resource concerns
* identify areas requiring decisions
* support preparation of Management Review agendas

Control Manifold Reports do not constitute:

* Management Review Records
* Management Review decisions
* approved actions
* policy changes
* objective changes
* controlled risk records
* approved resource allocations

All decisions and actions arising from Management Review shall be documented and controlled according to applicable SOPs and Work Instructions.

---

## **12 Relationship to Planning and Continual Improvement**

Information contained within Control Manifold Reports may result in:

* new plans
* planning adjustments
* Management Review actions
* improvement initiatives
* resource actions
* portfolio adjustments
* updated risks and opportunities
* changes to objectives
* changes to process documentation

Outputs shall be managed through applicable controlled processes.

## **14 Notes**

The Control Manifold intentionally separates:

**Operational Telemetry**
→ What is happening?

**Control Manifold**
→ What situation are we in?

**Management Review**
→ What should change?

This separation allows operational execution, situational understanding, and governance decisions to evolve independently while remaining fully connected and traceable through the QMS planning and review processes.
